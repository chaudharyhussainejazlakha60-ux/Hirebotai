Summary: We collect only what's necessary. We never sell your data.

1. Who We Are

HireBot AI is operated by Chaudhary Hussain Ejaz Lakha. Contact: chaudharyhussainejazlakha60@gmail.com

2. Data We Collect

Account data (name, email), interview recordings, AI scores, anti-cheat logs, usage data. Payment data is processed by Stripe — we never store card details.

3. How We Use It

To provide the service, send reports to HR, process payments, and improve the product. We do not use your data to train AI models without consent.

4. Video Recordings

Recordings are stored locally on your device. In paid plans with cloud storage, they are stored encrypted. Candidates are notified via the REC indicator.

5. Your Rights (GDPR)

Access, correct, delete your data, restrict processing, data portability. Email us to exercise any right — we respond within 30 days.

6. Third Parties

Google Gemini (AI scoring), EmailJS (email delivery), Stripe (payments), Supabase (auth + database). Minimum necessary data shared only.

Essential cookies only — session, preferences. No tracking or advertising cookies. See our Cookie Policy.

8. Data Retention

Account data kept while active. On deletion, all data removed within 30 days.
